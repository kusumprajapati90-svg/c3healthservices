Comprehensive Post-Operative Management, Vitals Monitoring & Safe Ambulation

Returning home after major surgery demands careful physical supervision, strict infection control measures, and structured assistance to ensure transitions are safe and free from injury risks.

Consistent checking and record-keeping of crucial body signals. Our post-surgical medical attendants monitor temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, and oxygen saturation parameters to quickly detect and notify family members of early recovery deviations.

Safe Mobility Support

Careful movement assistance to avoid physical strains or stitch breaks. Attendants provide stability for transfers from bed to chair, help with doctor-recommended post-op walking protocols, and ensure regular posture alterations to prevent bedsores.

Medication Management

Strict execution of post-surgical discharge prescription schedules. Caregivers track medicine timings, monitor nutritional intake, assist with high-fiber recovery diets, and support basic personal hygiene needs to keep the healing environment clean.
